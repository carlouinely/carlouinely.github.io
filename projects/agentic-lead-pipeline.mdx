## Outreach that starts from the site

Cold outreach is only as good as the list behind it. This internal pipeline at
Zaigo sources leads, scores their websites, and decides which prospects are worth
a closer look.

Prospects whose sites score low receive a custom mockup by email, so the first
contact already shows what their site could be. The sourcing, scoring, and
sending run on their own.
