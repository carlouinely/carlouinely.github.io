## From concept to launch

An artist needs to be found before they can be booked, and a portfolio that
search engines cannot read does not help. This site was built around
discoverability from the first wireframe rather than retrofitted afterwards.

Working with **DEVGO Studio**, I took the project from concept through to launch —
structure, content, and the search work that turns a portfolio into an entry
point for the artist's audience.
