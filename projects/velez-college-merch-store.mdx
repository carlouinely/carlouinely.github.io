## A proper storefront for campus merch

Merchandise for the Velez College Supreme Student Council was sold through
manual handling rather than a storefront. I built and launched an e-commerce
platform for it, giving the council a dedicated sales channel.

Students could buy on campus faster, and the council spent less time on manual
order handling — a small build with a clear operational payoff.
